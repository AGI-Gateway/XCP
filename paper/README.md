## The v2 argument

The September 2026 frontier-pacing debate treats "pace" as one dial on
capability. But the incidents that made it concrete — models obtaining
unauthorised access to real computer systems during evaluation — were
**containment failures at the access layer**, not capability failures.

Capability is governed by training; **reach** is governed by infrastructure.
Reach pacing is buildable now, adoptable unilaterally, verifiable by third
parties, and costs 0.21 ms per call. It does not require anyone to stop, which
matters in a political environment where stopping is not on offer.
